Irtiqa AI

Irtiqa AI is not an automation agency. Not a chatbot company. Not a CRM setup service. We are the infrastructure layer serious businesses build on when they want to scale without chaos — designing, building, and operating the operational and revenue infrastructure that stops silent revenue leakage.

Core capabilities

  • Revenue operations infrastructure: lead capture → qualification → booking → onboarding → retention
  • Multi-agent AI systems that run complex workflows end-to-end
  • Multi-model AI architecture for speed, accuracy, and cost efficiency
  • AI receptionist and front desk infrastructure (voice, chat, WhatsApp)
  • Autonomous lead generation, outreach, and appointment setting systems
  • CRM and pipeline automation with real-time reporting

Primary markets and buyer profiles

Irtiqa AI works with founder-led businesses, service teams, and operators who need a reliable growth machine. Engagements focus on commercially critical bottlenecks: slow response, inconsistent follow-up, weak onboarding, retention drops, and operational workflows that rely on people instead of systems.
