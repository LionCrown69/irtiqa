Frequently asked questions

What is revenue operations infrastructure?

Revenue operations infrastructure connects lead capture, qualification, follow-up, booking, onboarding, and reporting into one coordinated system. It reduces leakage between handoffs and makes conversion performance predictable from first inquiry to retained client.

How does Irtiqa AI improve revenue operations performance?

Irtiqa AI improves revenue operations by removing manual latency, standardizing workflow decisions, and building visibility into where money is leaking. We fix root causes and deploy the minimum viable infrastructure to raise conversion and retention without adding headcount.

Can AI revenue operations be customized by business type?

Yes. Irtiqa AI designs infrastructure around your business model, sales cycle, and delivery constraints. The workflow logic for clinics, agencies, consultancies, SaaS, and distribution models is different — the system must fit the operation, not the other way around.
