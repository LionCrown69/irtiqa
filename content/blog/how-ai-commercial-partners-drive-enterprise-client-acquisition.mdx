## The 3-Part Framework for Enterprise Client Acquisition

When an organization attempts to scale high-ticket service contracts ($10,000 to $100,000+ ACV), traditional sales tactics break down because they focus on *pitching* rather than *diagnosing*. 

Within the Irtiqa AI commercial ecosystem, Revenue Growth Partners utilize a structured 3-part framework to engage executive decision-makers:

### 1. Pre-Outreach Structural Research
Before a Revenue Growth Partner initiates contact with a prospective enterprise account, they do not simply look at a company name and job title. Operating through our private **Revenue Partner Operating System (Partner OS)**, partners analyze the target organization's digital footprint, technology stack, and operational indicators.

Key diagnostic questions include:
* Is the organization running fragmented CRM architectures (e.g., disconnected HubSpot and legacy ERP systems)?
* Are there demonstrable signs of **revenue leakage**, such as unoptimized inbound intake flows or delayed response velocity?
* What are the specific EBITDA expansion pressures facing this sector in the current financial quarter?

By entering the initial conversation with a pre-constructed hypothesis regarding the client's operational friction, the partner establishes immediate authority.

### 2. Diagnostic Qualification Over Feature Pitching
One of the core rules within the [Revenue Growth Partner Programme](/program) is that **we never pitch solutions on an initial discovery call.** 

When an RGP engages a C-suite leader, their sole objective is diagnostic qualification. We train our partners to act like specialized organizational physicians. An effective commercial partner asks probing, high-value questions:

> *"When a high-intent inbound enterprise inquiry arrives after hours on a Friday, precisely how many hours elapse before your technical team initiates a diagnostic conversation? What is the quantifiable revenue drop-off of that delay over a 12-month cycle?"*

This shift from vendor pitching to strategic diagnostic inquiry separates elite commercial partners from standard sales representatives.

### 3. Seamless Consultation Handoff & Deal Structuring
Once an enterprise prospect's revenue leakage and growth constraints have been accurately diagnosed and quantified, the opportunity transitions into the conversion phase.

Depending on the partner's experience tier and agreement structure:
* **The 20% Track (Appointment to Close):** The partner books a verified discovery consultation with Irtiqa AI’s senior consulting and engineering leadership. Our senior architects structure the technical roadmap, demonstrate the custom AI infrastructure build, and execute the closing agreement—awarding the partner a 20% commission on the closed contract.
* **The 30% Track (End-to-End Closing):** Advanced commercial leaders who independently source, lead the technical discovery, structure the commercial proposal, and secure client sign-off earn a 30% commission on the revenue generated.
