# The Diagnostic Approach to Revenue Operations

Too many agencies sell tools before they understand the problem. They recommend a CRM migrate, a new email sequence, or an AI receptionist before auditing where the real commercial leakage is occurring.

At Irtiqa AI, we believe in **diagnostic-first engineering**.

## Step 1: The Audit Call (One Hour, Maximum Depth)

We start by mapping your entire lead-to-client pipeline. We don't ask high-level questions; we look at the actual steps:
- How does a lead get routed when they fill out a form?
- What happens if they don't respond to the first follow-up?
- Who owns the task of updating the deal stage?
- How long does it take for an invoice to get sent after a contract is signed?

## Step 2: The Growth Report (24-Hour Delivery)

Within 24 hours of our call, you receive a detailed, customized blueprint. We show you:
1. **The Leakage Log**: The exact points where leads or clients are dropping out of your process.
2. **Commercial Estimates**: The monthly dollar value of the leaked revenue.
3. **The Minimum Viable Infrastructure (MVI)**: The smallest set of integrations, workflows, or agent deployments required to plug the leak.

## Step 3: Build & Operate

Once the roadmap is approved, we build the custom connectors, multi-agent flows, and pipeline trackers. But our work doesn't stop there. We run, monitor, and optimize these systems continuously, ensuring that as your traffic and sales grow, your infrastructure scales without breaking.
